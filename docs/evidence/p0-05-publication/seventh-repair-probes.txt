Scope and implementation:
- Preserved reviewer-seventh-failed.md verbatim and changed only the two O08
  proof gaps identified there.
- Raw GTSAM Marginals covariance is first required finite. Before any
  symmetrization, the test computes and reports the Frobenius antisymmetry norm
  and its documented tight bound
  `1e-12 * max(1, ||C||_F)`, and separately requires
  `C.isApprox(C.transpose(), 1e-12)`. Only then is `0.5*(C+C^T)` passed to the
  self-adjoint eigensolver and required to have strictly positive minimum
  eigenvalue.
- Every armed fault point receives a process-global monotonic nonzero nonce.
  Only the exact hit helper can atomically record point/nonce/hit/consumed and
  consume the arm. The terminal receipt and out-of-object debug sidecar carry
  that identity. A natural backend exception leaves hit=false and nonce=0, so
  it cannot enter the reached set even when its coarse boundary is
  `backend_update`.
- All eight points require arm identity, terminal identity and consumed
  sidecar identity to match before insertion. Both the reached-point set and
  reached-nonce set must have exactly eight elements. There is no
  catch/skip/retry.
- The sidecar is reset on arm/initialize, disarmed on successful or exceptional
  completion, and erased by the estimator destructor, preventing stale tokens
  across address reuse. No estimator data member or golden public layout was
  changed.
